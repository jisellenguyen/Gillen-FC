About Gillen FC

Who We Are — Jake Gillen

Gillen FC is a pre-professional soccer team based in the Seattle area. Led by former Seattle United players and captains Owen Albrecht and Nolan Todd, Gillen FC was established to honor the legacy of former coach, mentor, and role model Jake Gillen. Made up of high-level players currently competing for college programs across the country, the Gillen FC team comes together to carry forward Jake's passion, respect, and pride for the game.

Culture

Gillen FC brings a professional, player-driven approach to the game to promote player development and a positive, collaborative team culture.

Our League

The Cascadia Premier League is built on the foundation of top amateur adult leagues in the Pacific Northwest, providing current and former college players with high-level competition opportunities over the summer season.

Giving Back

Charities

As a fully volunteer-based organization, Gillen FC prides itself on giving back to our community. Supporting Gillen FC not only sustains our team and honors the legacy of coach Jake Gillen, but also supports our partner charity organizations. Through in-kind donations, the players, coaches, and staff of Gillen FC proudly support:

The Goodtimes Project

Based in Washington State and Alaska State, The Goodtimes Project was formed in late 2013 to carry on the 30-year legacy of Camp Goodtimes and to ensure no-cost camps, events, and programs remained available to local families dealing with the financial and emotional costs of childhood cancer.

Camp Goodtimes

Camp Goodtimes provides a unique summer camp for children and young adults facing pediatric cancer. Built around a supportive and welcoming community, Camp Goodtimes aims to help kids flourish and revitalize the joys of childhood.

Water1st International

Water1st International's mission is to implement and sustain community-managed projects that integrate clean water supply, sanitation, and hygiene education for women and girls around the world.

Special Olympics

Special Olympics provides opportunities for year-round sports training and athletic competition in a variety of Olympic-type sports for children and adults with intellectual disabilities.

Support the Club

Donations

  • Donations to Gillen FC LLC are greatly appreciated and critical to the sustainability of the organization.
  • Donations are directly applied to the essential functions including but not limited to league and player registration fees, training and match space, equipment and kits.
  • Gillen FC is a fully volunteer organization — players, coaches, and staff are not compensated.
  • Support of Gillen FC not only sustains and promotes adult amateur soccer as well as honors the legacy of coach Jake Gillen, your donation supports our partner charity organizations.
  • Through in-kind donations of volunteer labor the players, coaches and staff of Gillen FC proudly support:
    • Camp Good Times
    • Water1st
    • Special Olympics

Donations made to Gillen FC LLC are not tax-deductible.
